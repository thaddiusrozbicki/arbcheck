Rules

Kalshi rules and Polymarket rules

Both resolve on the FOMC decision at the Dec 8 to 9, 2026 meeting, and the brackets match. A 50 bp hike is No for this market on both. Polymarket measures the change in the upper bound and rounds odd sizes up to the nearest 25 bp, so a 12.5 bp hike counts as 25. Kalshi's summary doesn't say how it treats odd sizes. If the meeting doesn't happen on its scheduled date, Kalshi resolves to "maintains rate" right away, while Polymarket waits until the next scheduled meeting, so a rescheduled meeting could split them.
